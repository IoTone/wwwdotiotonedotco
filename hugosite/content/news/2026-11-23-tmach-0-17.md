[**TMach**](https://github.com/IoTone/Telemachus) (Telemachus), our self-hosted AI workspace for teams, has shipped five versions since the [0.12.0 security release](/news/2026-11-13-telemachus-0-12/): 0.14.0 on November 16 through 0.17.1 on November 22, about a hundred commits in all.

## A clearer pitch

The README now opens with one sentence: **private AI for your workgroup, running on hardware you own.**

The rewrite spells out who TMach is for. Hosted assistants are easy but send everything your team types to someone else's servers. Local tools keep data at home but are mostly built for one person at one computer. TMach is for the case in between: a group sharing private AI on shared hardware, which needs accounts, permissions, limits and a record of who did what. It is MIT licensed, with no per-seat fees and no paid edition.

The README also gained deployment blueprints, topology diagrams and a feature matrix.

## Email that leaves the building (0.15.0)

Until now TMach's email was for its own users: confirmations, resets and notifications. It can now write to people outside the company.

- **External recipients.** A message goes to an outside contact from a template. Deleting the contact erases the address.
- **Signed mail.** Messages are signed so that they pass DMARC checks at the receiving end.
- **Replies have somewhere to go.** One optional inbound mailbox means a reply address is no longer a dead end.
- **In the reader's language.** Notifications, confirmations, resets and invitations are composed in the recipient's own language.

## Notes that know where they came from (0.15.0, 0.16.0)

- A note can **link to its sources**, and a bookmark or document lists the notes that cite it.
- Markdown notes have links.
- A note can be marked as a **template**.
- Two **tidying lists** show notes nothing links to and links that go nowhere.

## Research and the knowledge graph (0.17.0, 0.17.1)

- **Bookmarks re-fetch on a timer**, set from the Research tab, so a saved page does not go stale.
- **Plugins can lend their own documents to the knowledge graph.** A plugin that holds records of its own can now have them read into the same graph as the team's documents.
- **A more forgiving extractor.** When the model's reply contained one bad item, the whole document used to be refused. The extractor now keeps every item that passes.

One result is worth reporting because it was negative. The team measured whether expanding a search through the knowledge graph improved recall over plain retrieval. It did not, and the write-up says so.

## Tighter by default (0.14.0, 0.15.0)

The follow-ups from November's security audit continued:

- A route may declare the shape of its request body, and a declared shape is enforced. A caller sending the wrong one gets a clear 400.
- No default password is published anywhere, in the documentation or the sign-in form.
- An operator can issue a **machine token** for a service account.
- A request forwarded by a proxy is no longer treated as a local caller, and per-address limits see the real caller behind a trusted proxy.

## Getting it

Versions 0.14.0 to 0.17.1 are tagged in the repository. If you run an instance, 0.17.1 is the one to install. Source, the feature tour and the free e-book are at [github.com/IoTone/Telemachus](https://github.com/IoTone/Telemachus).
