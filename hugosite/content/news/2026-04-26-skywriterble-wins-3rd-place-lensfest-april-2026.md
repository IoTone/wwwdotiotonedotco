A share on one of IoTone, Inc.'s XR prototype projects turned into a Lensfest hackathon win this month. The April 2026 Lensfest Spectacles Community Challenge — run by [Lenslist](https://lenslist.co) and [Snap Inc.](https://snap.com) — awarded [Spectacles-SkywriterBLE](https://github.com/IoTone/Spectacles-SkywriterBLE) 3rd Place in the OSS category.

The lens is a distraction-free word processor for XR. Tiny note-taking and journaling tools are something we keep reaching for in spatial computing — popping up a clean text surface on demand, no chrome, no UI noise, is a huge convenience inside a headset. SkywriterBLE pairs a real BLE HID keyboard to Spectacles so the typing experience matches what you already have on your desk, then renders the text into a quiet floating surface in front of you.

This is the second Lensfest placement in a row for IoTone, Inc., following [Polynode's 2nd Place win in March 2026](/news/2026-03-30-polynode-wins-2nd-place-lensfest-march-2026/). Both projects are MIT-licensed; source is on [github.com/IoTone](https://github.com/IoTone).
