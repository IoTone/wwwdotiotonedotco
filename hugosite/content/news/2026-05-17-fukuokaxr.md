IoTone Japan stood up [**fukuokaxr.org**](https://fukuokaxr.org/) — a bilingual (JA / EN) landing page for **福岡の XR / AR / VR コミュニティ**, Fukuoka's XR / AR / VR community.

It's a deliberately minimal, single-page site: the standing home for the local scene that IoTone Japan has been building around events like [XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka](/news/2026-04-30-xrvdc-fukuoka-site-live/) and [VisionDevCamp Fukuoka](/news/2025-02-26-visiondevcamp-fukuoka/). Source (a small Hugo site) is on [github.com/IoTone](https://github.com/IoTone/fukuokaxr).
