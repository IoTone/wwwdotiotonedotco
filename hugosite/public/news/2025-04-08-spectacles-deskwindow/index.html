SpectaclesDeskWindow ships — first OSS spatial desktop mirror

Spatial desktop mirroring solution for Snap Spectacles AR glasses — the first OSS solution for streaming a desktop into AR.

News · April 8, 2025 · 1 min read

SpectaclesDeskWindow, created by IoTone Japan, is a mirroring solution for the Snap Spectacles AR glasses. It’s the first OSS solution for remotely viewing your desktop (or a desktop-attached camera) in a spatial context on Spectacles. Source is on GitHub.
