Spectacles SkywriterBLE — typing in AR

A BLE HID keyboard reference for Snap Spectacles, with a prototype word processor on top. Solves typing in AR.

News · April 8, 2026 · 1 min read

Spectacles-SkywriterBLE is a BLE HID Keyboard example for Snap Spectacles, with a prototype word processor that demonstrates real text entry in AR. A reference for anyone trying to make Spectacles a productive surface.
