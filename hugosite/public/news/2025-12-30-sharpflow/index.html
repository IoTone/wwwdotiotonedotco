SharpFlow: drag-and-drop ML pipeline (no Python required)

A simple app pipeline for using ML.NET (Sharp) in a drag-and-drop splat pipeline, for people who don't need Python in their lives.

News · December 30, 2025 · 1 min read

SharpFlow is a Rust-based app pipeline that uses ML Sharp in a drag-and-drop “splat” workflow — for makers who want to compose ML pipelines without bringing the entire Python ecosystem along for the ride.
