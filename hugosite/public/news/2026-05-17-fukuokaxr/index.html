fukuokaxr.org — a home for Fukuoka's XR community

IoTone Japan stood up fukuokaxr.org, a bilingual (JA/EN) landing page for Fukuoka's XR / AR / VR community — the standing home behind events like XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka.

News · May 17, 2026 · 1 min read

IoTone Japan stood up fukuokaxr.org — a bilingual (JA / EN) landing page for 福岡の XR / AR / VR コミュニティ, Fukuoka’s XR / AR / VR community.

It’s a deliberately minimal, single-page site: the standing home for the local scene that IoTone Japan has been building around events like XR VisionDevCamp Fukuoka and VisionDevCamp Fukuoka. Source (a small Hugo site) is on github.com/IoTone.
